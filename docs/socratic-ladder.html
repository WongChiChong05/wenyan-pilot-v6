Socratic Ladder

How the questioning arm decides when a reader is allowed to see the explanation — and why a correct answer alone is not enough.

The three rungs

A reader in the questioning arm clicks one of the four target sentences. Every rung has the same shape: a three-option multiple choice, plus a written reason. The pick is recognition; the reason is production. The reader never faces a blank box asking what the answer is — only why they chose it.

Why a correct pick is not enough

A three-option choice is right one time in three by luck. If a correct pick alone opened the explanation, roughly a third of readers would skip the ladder entirely — and for them the questioning manipulation would simply not have happened. The reason gate is the only thing standing between the study and that hole.

The gate judges whether a reason exists, not whether it is right. A wrong but reasoned answer passes. 「不知道」, 「猜的」 and bare echoes of an option do not. Unclear cases go to the model. Two rejections per rung is the budget; the third attempt is forced through and flagged reasonInsufficient, so a reader can be slowed down but never stuck.

A worked sentence

Target sentence 「以鐵如意擊之，應手而碎」 — the language point is 「之」 as a pronoun. Rungs 1 and 2 ask about observations: what part of speech sits beside the word, where the struck object appears. Neither can give the layer-3 answer away, and the content validator rejects any rung whose options duplicate it.

LAYER 1

先看這一句裡的「之」。它前面緊接著的「擊」，是一個動作還是一個東西？

  • 一個動作（敲、打）
  • 一個東西（器物）
  • 一個形容的詞

and: why did you pick that? — getting it wrong is fine

LAYER 2

「擊」是一個動作。那麼「被敲的東西」在這句話裡出現在哪裡？

  • 在「擊」的前面
  • 在「擊」的後面
  • 這句話裡沒有寫出來
LAYER 3

「擊之」的「之」在這句話裡的作用比較像哪一個？

  • 的
  • 指某個東西
  • 往、去
EXPLAIN

「之」是代詞，指珊瑚樹，接在動作「擊」後面，是被敲的對象。

What separates the three arms

The ladder is the whole manipulation. Strip it out and you have the direct arm; strip out the response as well and you have the control arm. Everything else — the passage, the annotations, the reading column width — is held constant.

Invariants worth defending

  1. 01 The model never decides when the explanation appears. A server-side state machine does — src/lib/ladder.ts. The model only writes the text for the rung the machine is already on, so “the explanation came too early” is impossible by construction rather than by prompt discipline.
  2. 02 A correct pick alone never opens the explanation. The reason must pass too, or a one-in-three guess would skip the ladder.
  3. 03 Both AI arms receive the identical explanation. Not paraphrased, not regenerated — the same stored string. The questioning arm reaching it later is the manipulation; reaching something different would be a confound.
  4. 04 Rungs 1 and 2 ask about observations, never meaning. The content validator fails the build if a rung's options duplicate the layer-3 answer options.
  5. 05 Where the reader stopped is the record, not just whether they were right. endPosition is one of layer1_correct, layer2_correct, choice_correct, choice_wrong — how far down the ladder each sentence had to go.
  6. 06 Nobody can be trapped. Two reason rejections per rung, then forced through and flagged. A stuck reader is a dropout, and a pilot of fifteen per arm cannot afford one.
